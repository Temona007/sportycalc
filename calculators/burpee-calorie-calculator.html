Burpee Calorie Calculator: How Many Calories Do Burpees Burn?

Burpees are one of the most effective full-body exercises — but exactly how many calories do they burn? Our Burpee Calorie Calculator gives you a clear, science-based estimate based on your body weight and the number of burpees performed. Whether you're doing a quick set or a full burpee workout, this tool helps you understand the real energy cost.

How the Burpee Calorie Calculator Works

The calculator uses a proven estimation method:

  • Reference: A 150 lb (68 kg) person burns approximately 0.5 kcal per burpee on average.
  • It then scales the result based on your body weight.
  • You get three estimates:
    • Low — slow pace, easier form
    • Average — standard effort
    • High — fast pace, full effort with jump and push-up

Inputs

  • Your body weight (kg or lbs)
  • Number of burpees performed

Output

  • Total calories burned (low / average / high)
  • Calories per burpee

Example Calculations

Example 1: Weight: 70 kg (154 lbs) | Burpees: 20

  • Low: ~5.1 kcal
  • Average: ~10.3 kcal
  • High: ~15.4 kcal

Example 2: Weight: 85 kg (187 lbs) | Burpees: 50

  • Average: ~32.5 kcal (roughly the same as running half a mile)

Why Burpees Are So Effective

Burpees combine strength, cardio, and explosiveness in one movement. They burn calories during the exercise and keep your metabolism elevated afterward (EPOC effect).

Benefits:

  • Full-body workout (legs, core, chest, shoulders)
  • Improves cardiovascular fitness
  • No equipment needed
  • Great for fat loss and conditioning

Tips to Maximize Calorie Burn with Burpees

  • Focus on proper form for safety and better results
  • Add burpees to HIIT circuits for higher calorie burn
  • Combine with strength training for balanced fitness
  • Track your total weekly volume using this calculator
