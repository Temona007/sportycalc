Waist-to-Height Ratio Calculator: Check Your Health Risk Instantly

Your Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR) is a simple but powerful indicator of health risk. Research shows it often predicts cardiovascular and metabolic risk better than BMI alone because it focuses on abdominal fat.

Our free Waist-to-Height Ratio Calculator uses the widely recognized Ashwell shape chart to give you a clear category and practical waist targets.

How the Calculator Works

Formula:

WHtR = Waist ÷ Height

(Use the same units for both measurements — cm or inches.)

Ashwell Shape Chart Categories

WHtR Range Category Meaning
Below 0.40Take CarePossibly underweight
0.40 – 0.49OKHealthy range
0.50 – 0.59Consider ActionIncreased health risk
0.60 and aboveTake ActionHigh health risk

Important rule of thumb: Keep your waist circumference below half of your height (WHtR < 0.50).

What You’ll Get

  • Your exact Waist-to-Height Ratio
  • Health risk category
  • Maximum recommended waist size (0.5 × height)
  • Ideal healthy waist range (0.40 – 0.49 × height)

Example

  • Waist: 71 cm
  • Height: 170 cm
  • WHtR = 0.42 → OK
  • Keep waist below 85.0 cm
  • Healthy waist range: 68.0 – 83.3 cm

Why Waist-to-Height Ratio Matters

  • Better indicator of abdominal (visceral) fat than BMI
  • Strongly linked to heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic syndrome risk
  • Easy to track over time with just a measuring tape
  • Useful for both men and women (same cut-offs apply)

How to Measure Correctly

  • Stand upright and relax
  • Measure your waist at the midpoint between the bottom of your ribs and the top of your hip bone (usually around the navel)
  • Keep the tape snug but not tight
  • Measure height without shoes

Tips to Improve Your WHtR

  • Focus on reducing abdominal fat through a moderate calorie deficit
  • Prioritize strength training + Zone 2 cardio
  • Improve sleep and manage stress
  • Track progress weekly under the same conditions
