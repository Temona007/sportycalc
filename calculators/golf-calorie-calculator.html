Golf may not look like a high-intensity workout, but walking 18 holes while carrying clubs can burn a surprising number of calories.

Our free Golf Calorie Calculator helps you estimate exactly how many calories you burn based on your body weight, playing time, and how you move around the course.

How the Golf Calorie Calculator Works

The calculator uses the standard MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) formula:

Calories = MET × Body Weight (kg) × Duration (hours)

MET values used:

Playing Style MET Value
Carrying clubs4.3
Pulling a cart4.5
Riding a cart3.5
General / Mixed4.5

Inputs

  • Body weight (kg or lbs)
  • Duration (hours + minutes)
  • Playing mode (carrying, pulling, riding, or mixed)

Outputs

  • Total calories burned
  • Calories per hour
  • Comparison vs riding a cart

Quick Presets

  • 9 holes → 2 hours 30 minutes
  • 18 holes → 4 hours 30 minutes
  • Practice session → 1 hour 30 minutes

Example Calculations

  • 80 kg, 4.5 hours, carrying clubs → 1,548 kcal
  • 70 kg, 4 hours, riding a cart → 980 kcal

Why Tracking Golf Calories Matters

  • Helps you understand the real energy cost of your rounds
  • Useful for weight management and nutrition planning
  • Shows the difference between walking and riding
  • Motivates more active play (carrying or pulling instead of riding)

Tips to Burn More Calories on the Course

  • Walk instead of riding whenever possible
  • Carry your bag or use a push/pull cart
  • Play longer courses or add a practice session
  • Stay consistent with regular rounds
