Triathlon Calculator: Predict Your Race Time with Precision

Planning a triathlon? Whether you’re racing a Sprint, Olympic, Half-Ironman, or full Ironman, knowing your expected finish time is essential for pacing and goal setting.

Our free Triathlon Calculator lets you estimate your total race time by combining swim, bike, and run performance — including transition times (T1 and T2).

How the Triathlon Calculator Works

Key features:

  • Unit Toggle — Switch between Metric and Imperial
  • Race Presets — Sprint, Olympic, Half-Ironman (70.3), and Ironman (140.6)
  • Custom Distances — Enter your own swim, bike, and run distances
  • Live Pace ↔ Time Conversion
    • Swim: pace (min/100m or min/100yd) ↔ split time
    • Bike: speed (km/h or mph) ↔ split time
    • Run: pace (min/km or min/mi) ↔ split time
  • Transition Times — Add T1 (swim to bike) and T2 (bike to run)
  • Real-time Results — Total time updates automatically

What You’ll See in the Results

  • Total estimated race time
  • Individual split times (Swim, T1, Bike, T2, Run)
  • Your swim pace, bike speed, and run pace

Why Use This Triathlon Calculator?

  • Set realistic race goals
  • Plan pacing strategy across all three disciplines
  • Experiment with different swim, bike, and run performances
  • Account for transition times (often overlooked)
  • Useful for both beginners and experienced triathletes

Pro Tips for Better Predictions

  • Use recent training data for more accurate inputs
  • Be conservative with transition times if you’re new to racing
  • Account for course conditions (hills, wind, open water)
  • Practice transitions in training — they can save valuable minutes
