Key Calculations Explained

Implied Probability

Shows the break-even win percentage the odds imply. Example: +150 American odds = 40% implied probability.

Profit & Payout

  • Profit = Stake × (Decimal Odds – 1)
  • Total Payout = Stake × Decimal Odds

Parlay Calculation

All legs are converted to decimal odds, then multiplied together to get the combined odds.
