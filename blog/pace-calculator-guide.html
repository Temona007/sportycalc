What Is Pace and Why Does It Matter?

Pace is the time it takes to cover a specific distance — usually expressed as minutes per kilometer (min/km) or minutes per mile (min/mi).

For example:

  • A pace of 5:30 min/km means you run one kilometer in 5 minutes and 30 seconds.
  • A pace of 8:45 min/mi means one mile in 8 minutes and 45 seconds.

Unlike speed (km/h or mph), pace is easier to understand during training. It tells you exactly how fast you should be moving right now to hit your goal.

Popular Training Paces You Should Know

  • Easy Run Pace — Comfortable, conversational pace (usually Zone 2)
  • Tempo Pace — Comfortably hard, sustainable for 20–60 minutes
  • Interval / Speed Pace — Hard effort for short bursts
  • Long Run Pace — Slightly slower than easy pace for building endurance

Using the right pace for each workout is one of the biggest keys to improvement without getting injured.

Pro Tips for Better Results with Pace Training

  • Start most runs in easy pace (Zone 2) — this builds your aerobic base
  • Use pace targets during races instead of constantly checking your watch
  • Adjust pace based on terrain, weather, and fatigue
  • Track your progress monthly — you should see your easy pace getting faster over time
