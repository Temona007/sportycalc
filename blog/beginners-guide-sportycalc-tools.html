Beginner's Guide to Using All SportyCalc Tools (Complete Roadmap)

Starting a fitness journey can feel overwhelming. There are so many numbers — calories, macros, heart rate zones, body fat, PT scores… That's exactly why we built SportyCalc — a complete set of free, easy-to-use calculators that remove the guesswork from fitness. This beginner's guide will walk you through all the most important tools on SportyCalc and show you the best order to use them. Think of it as your personal roadmap.

Step 1: Understand Your Body's Needs

Start Here:

TDEE Calculator

This is the foundation. It tells you exactly how many calories your body burns every day (maintenance level). → TDEE Calculator

Next:

Macro Calculator

Once you know your TDEE, this tool splits your calories into protein, carbs, and fats based on your goal (fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance). → Macro Calculator

Step 2: Know Where You Stand Right Now

Fitness Age Calculator

Find out if your body is biologically younger or older than your real age. → Fitness Age Calculator

Body Fat Percentage Calculator + Army Body Fat Calculator

Get accurate body composition measurements. → Body Fat Percentage Calculator • Army Body Fat Calculator

Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR)

A simple but powerful health indicator. → Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator

Step 3: Train Smarter, Not Harder

Target Heart Rate Calculator + Heart Rate Zone 2 Guide

Learn the best heart rate zones for fat burning and endurance. → Target Heart Rate Calculator • Heart Rate Zone 2 Guide

Pace Calculator

Perfect for runners and anyone doing timed workouts. → Running Pace Calculator

Step 4: Military & Performance Tools

  • Air Force PT Calculator
  • Army Body Fat Calculator

These are excellent even if you're not in the military — they set very high, realistic standards.

Step 5: Recovery & Lifestyle Tools

Sleep Cycle Calculator

One of the most underrated tools. Better sleep = faster progress. → Sleep Cycle Calculator

Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) Calculator

Understand how alcohol really affects your recovery. → BAC Calculator

Recommended Beginner Roadmap (First 30 Days)

WeekFocus
Week 1Run TDEE + Macro Calculator + Fitness Age
Week 2Measure Body Fat % and WHR
Week 3Start using Heart Rate Zones during cardio
Week 4Add Sleep Cycle Calculator and track improvements

Final Tips for Beginners

  • Don't try to use every tool at once. Start with 2–3.
  • Track your numbers every 4 weeks — progress is motivating!
  • All tools are 100% free with no sign-up required.
  • Combine the calculators — they work best together.

Fitness doesn't have to be complicated. With the right data, small consistent changes lead to big results.
